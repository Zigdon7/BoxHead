What is power?

Every piece of gear has a power from 1 to 100: how good it is for its tier. Loot from harder maps and rarer drops has more. Your gear score is the average power of your 4 slots, and it decides which maps you can enter. Stats like DPS or health are what the item does in a fight; power is what it's worth for your gear score.
