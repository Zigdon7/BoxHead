A co-op extraction shooter built from classic BoxHead: readable top-down combat, huge infested cities, and a stash that only grows if you make it back.

Gear up

Your stash is your progress.

Every gun, vest and pack has a power rating. Your gear score, the average of your loadout, decides which of the four maps you can enter. The trader sells the basics; the good stuff only comes out of raids.

Raid

Big maps, real risk.

Each map is a city of buildings, streets and monster nests that can't be destroyed. Loot crates, chain kills for bounty, and stay on the move. Linger too long and an Abomination comes looking for you.

Extract

Nothing is yours until you're out.

Reach a green extraction point before your raid clock runs out to keep your loadout, loot and bounty. Die, run out of time or leave, and everything you carried in is gone.

The ladder

Outskirts is open to everyone. Rust Yard needs gear score 20, Quarantine Zone 40 and Black Site 65. Each map's common loot stops just short of the next gate, so a few good extractions (and a lucky rare) move you up.

Shared raids, server-run.

The server runs every raid, so squads always see the same world. Raids are persistent: you can drop into one that's already running, and each operator has their own raid clock. Sign in with Steam, Bluesky, Google or email: your stash lives on your account, on any device.
