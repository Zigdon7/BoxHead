Small squad.
Very big problem.

A multiplayer rebuild inspired by classic BoxHead: readable top-down action, increasingly dangerous waves, and just enough time to reload your plan.

Enter the arena → Read the field guide
Fight

Make every kill count.

Chain kills for combo rewards, switch between four firearms, and carve an escape with the Plasma Katana. Earn credits to upgrade your build without spending your score.

Build

Own your ground.

Funnel enemies into barricades, seed approaches with claymores, and turn explosive barrels into a chain reaction. When the horde closes in, dash or escape through the edge tunnels.

Survive

Keep the squad alive.

Revive downed teammates, resupply during wave breaks, and use the host’s shared pause when you need a breather. Retry unlocked waves as your tactics improve.

Know what you’re up against.

Five enemy types have distinct movement patterns and strengths. Learn to spot Brutes by their heavy brown bodies and red snouts; their attacks are close-range, not invisible projectiles.

One arena. One shared simulation.

The server runs the combat and keeps the squad in the same world. The first player to join is the host, with exclusive pause/resume control. Pausing freezes the round for everyone; opening a shop or guide alone does not.

Wave records persist on the server under a browser-specific identity. Weapons, upgrades and credits reset each round. For the complete controls, arsenal and pickup guide, visit Learn to Play.
