Your first 60 seconds

01 / Keep moving

Make space

WASD moves, your mouse aims, and left-click shoots. Walk backward while aiming into the horde. The pistol never runs out of ammunition.

02 / Escape pressure

Dash & cleave

Shift dashes with brief invulnerability. Space or right-click swings the katana. Melee kills restore health. Don’t let a Brute corner you.

03 / Spend wisely

Rearm between waves

B opens the armory. Buy with credits, not score. Wave breaks last 8 seconds; the shop itself does not pause combat.

Controls shown are defaults. Rebind actions in Settings. On touch devices, use the movement stick and on-screen attack/weapon controls. The first connected player is the host and can pause everyone with P or the Pause button.

Threat recognition

Five faces of the horde

Enemy health and speed scale with the wave. All five types attack in melee—none fires projectiles. First appearance is the earliest wave that type can spawn.

Personal arsenal

Four firearms. One very sharp backup.

Base damage is before upgrades and critical hits. Firearms share your ammo reserve; the pistol is unlimited. Select unlocked guns with 1–4.

Shape the battlefield

Tactical gear

Begin each round with 2 barrels, 2 barricades and 1 claymore. Deploy with E/Q/F, or 5/6/7. Explosives chain-react.

Field resupply

Don’t leave it on the floor.

Walk over a pickup to collect it. Enemy drops expire after 12 seconds; fixed ammo stations refill after 15 seconds.

Spend credits. Keep your score.

The tactical armory

Press B, then click a card or use its displayed number. Upgrades last for this round, not across restarts. Start with $100; kills, combos and wave clears earn more.

Survive together

Squad rules & survival tricks

Host pause

The first connected player controls Pause/Resume (P). Simulation, attacks, spawns and wave timers stop for everyone. Only the host can resume. If they leave, the game resumes and the longest-connected remaining player becomes host.

Bring them back

Stand within 50 pixels of a downed teammate for 3 seconds to revive them with 50 HP. The squad loses when nobody is left standing. The death recap lists recent server-recorded hits.

Chain your kills

Kill again within 4 seconds to keep your combo alive. Score multiplier grows up to 5×. A 10-kill combo activates Overdrive: +15% movement speed and faster attacks. Wave breaks preserve your combo timer.

Use the tunnels

The glowing openings at the center of each map edge wrap you to the opposite side. Use them to escape a surround—but remember enemies can wrap too.

Choose a starting wave

Explore the world map to choose an unlocked wave checkpoint. Clear your current checkpoint to open the next step. Be present before the first kill and stay through the clear to earn credit; mid-wave joiners qualify from the next wave. Late joiners enter the active wave but do not skip the route. Progress is server-saved for this browser.
